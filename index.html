Privacy Policy — Chance

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy applies to Chance, the Android game developed by ChessWind. This revision describes the data practices reviewed on October 26, 2026, including distribution through Google Play testing and Google Play Games features. It does not describe planned advertising, purchases, or other unreleased features as active services.

Game data on your device

Chance stores game progress and preferences on your device so you can continue playing. This includes language and audio settings, selected dice and other cosmetic items, unlocked items and in-game currency, roll and cashout totals, scores, achievements, current runs, and run history. The game no longer offers an editable player-name field. Existing saves and history entries can still retain a player-name field from earlier versions or its default value. Play Games uses its own gamer profile for its services. Saves also contain run identifiers, timestamps and recovery/synchronization records. Locally generated save-profile and synchronization identifiers, together with your game-scoped Play Games player identifier when available, help separate progress and associate synchronization with the appropriate account.

Local play does not require a separate email/password account. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permissions.

Google Play Games and cloud synchronization

Chance integrates Google Play Games Services. Play Games can attempt to authenticate your existing Google Play Games profile when the game starts. When sign-in and connectivity are available, Chance uses your game-scoped Play Games player identifier to associate game data with that account.

Saved Games sends game saves and run history, including the progress, retained player-name fields and preferences described above, to Google's service for backup, restoration and synchronization. Scores and achievement progress can also be sent to Google during gameplay; this is not limited to opening the leaderboard screen. Offline changes can remain on the device and be sent after connectivity and the appropriate signed-in identity become available.

Google processes your Play Games gamer identity and service diagnostics/usage data under its own policies. Leaderboard scores and Play Games profile information can be visible to other players according to Google's profile and leaderboard settings. Google documents HTTPS encryption in transit for Play Games Services data. See the Google Privacy Policy and Play Games Services data disclosures.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

The reviewed testing release includes Google advertising, consent and Play Billing libraries, but advertising, rewarded ads and real-money purchases are disabled in Chance. The game does not currently request or show ads, or offer a live purchase flow. We will revise this policy for changes to these practices before enabling them in a distributed version.

No separate ChessWind gameplay-telemetry upload service is enabled. This does not mean that Google services collect no data: their account, service, diagnostic and usage processing is described above and in Google's policies.

Support and external links

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots, files or diagnostic information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to investigate and respond to your request. Opening the support button opens your email application; it does not automatically send a message or attach your game saves. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or payment-card details.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it in a browser creates a request to that hosting service, which processes connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External Google pages and your email provider have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is retained for continued play rather than expiring after a fixed number of days. Temporary caches and superseded recovery copies can be replaced or removed as the game manages storage. Clearing Android's cache is different from clearing the app's storage/data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress, but does not by itself delete Google-held game data; a later sign-in or platform restore can restore data.

You can manage or delete Chance's Google Play Games data through your Play Games profile using Google's game-data deletion instructions. Google controls retention and deletion within its services. Deleting Play Games data does not necessarily delete a separate local copy, and signing out does not delete either copy. To avoid a retained copy being synchronized again, consider both local app data and the Google-held copy when requesting or performing deletion.

The current game does not provide a single button that erases every local and cloud copy. Contact us below for help with access, correction or deletion of data associated with Chance. Support correspondence is separate from your game save and is not deleted by clearing app data. Contact us to request its deletion; we will explain any applicable retention or deletion limits, including data controlled by Google. Do not send your Google password.
